## Resolved decisions (HORDE_PLAN §9)
- C2 kill counts: count INDIVIDUAL BODIES (big kill numbers). Missions/achievement thresholds must be rescaled so pacing stays balanced.
- C3 loot: per-body rolls allowed but horde loot is LOW VALUE and COMMON; total loot value per wave stays balanced (conserved/limited).
- Bit-identical: only required where design is unchanged. NEW RULE: enemies target and attack buildings standing in their way first, destroying them before reaching the core (buildings get HP; destroyed buildings are lost for the run). Report gate deltas instead of bit-identity once behavior changes.
- C8 fixed-length substeps: introduce in Phase 3.
- C4/C5 split rules: flat armor split per body proportionally; shields stay whole on elites (elites/bosses single bodies).
- Owner says "Implement horde features now": proceed through the horde phases without stopping for approval between them, reporting each phase's gates/deltas/profile in design/HORDE_PROGRESS.md.
